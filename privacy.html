Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of what this static arcade does and does not collect.

Information we collect

Mini Game Arcade is designed to work without an account. The games may store scores, personal-best records, settings, or game progress in your browser using local storage. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to us by the games. You can clear it through your browser settings; doing so may reset saved game records or progress.

If you contact us by email, your email provider will process the message and the information you choose to include.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and, where permitted by your settings and applicable law, personalize ads. These technologies may process information such as your browser, device, IP address, and interactions with ads. You can manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings and learn how Google uses information at Google advertising policies.

We do not operate our own analytics service. Some data may be processed by Google as part of its advertising services.

Children and privacy

The site does not ask visitors to submit personal information to play. Please do not include sensitive personal information in feedback messages.
