Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of what this static arcade does and does not collect.

Information we collect

Mini Game Arcade is designed to work without an account. The Fruit Slice game stores your personal-best score in your browser using local storage. That score stays on your device and is not sent to us by the game.

If you contact us by email, your email provider will process the message and the information you choose to include.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not intentionally set advertising cookies or include analytics trackers. It uses system fonts and locally stored artwork, and does not request third-party font files.

Children and privacy

The site does not ask visitors to submit personal information to play. Please do not include sensitive personal information in feedback messages.

Changes

This notice may be updated when site features or hosting arrangements change. The date of the latest update should be reviewed before public launch.
